# Asset provenance

All 15 equipment illustrations in `web/public/images/*.svg` are original vector
still lifes authored for this repository in `scripts/generate-art.mjs`.
Creator: Ammaar's Fieldroom project, with Codex assistance. Source: the generator
in this repository; license: [MIT](../LICENSE). No third-party artwork, tracing,
logos, photographs or models are incorporated. Product names/specifications are
fictional. Illustrations are interpretations, not photographs of real inventory.
Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-art.mjs`.

The interface uses **Schibsted Grotesk**, designed by Henrik Kongsvoll at
Bakken & Bæck for Schibsted. The unmodified variable WOFF2 is redistributed under
the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**, separately from the repository's MIT license:

- Source: [official Schibsted repository](https://github.com/schibsted/schibsted-grotesk),
  revision `d485f61f105e1b3935f4d21dfb4d371359798603`.
- Original path: `fonts/variable/SchibstedGrotesk[wght].woff2`.
- Local filename: `web/public/fonts/schibsted-grotesk-variable.woff2` (70,444 bytes).
- SHA-256: `8b77a34cdd59455aa3434e949597e99346204d40357905fd918df20ed1139ac1`.
- [Original copyright and OFL text](../web/public/fonts/OFL.txt) and
  [authors file](../web/public/fonts/AUTHORS.txt) are included unchanged.
- No glyph modifications or subsetting. The filename is simplified; the internal
  family name is unchanged. Loaded from the same origin, with no font-service requests.

Arial/sans-serif fallbacks and system monospace for asset identifiers require no
redistributed files. The borrow/return UI mark is an original inline SVG.
No GLB, WebGL, external images or remotely hosted assets. UI screenshots are generated
from this local application with fictional records only and contain no credentials.
Design references are documented in [the design notes](DESIGN.md); no reference-site
artwork, logos, stylesheets or layouts are copied.
